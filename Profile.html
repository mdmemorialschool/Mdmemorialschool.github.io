About Our School

M.D Memorial School

M.D Memorial School is an English-medium school located in Aurangabad, Bulandshahr, Uttar Pradesh.

Established in 1997, the school is committed to providing students with a supportive and engaging learning environment.

We focus on strong academic foundations, discipline, creativity, confidence and the overall development of every student.

Our Motto:
LEARN • GROW • SHINE

Our Educational Approach

We believe that education should go beyond textbooks. Students are encouraged to understand concepts, participate in activities and develop confidence.

Our approach combines classroom learning with activities, competitions, games, cultural programs and additional academic support.

Special attention is given to students who need additional academic support so that every learner gets an opportunity to progress.

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To create a learning environment where students develop knowledge, confidence, discipline, creativity and the skills required to face the future with confidence.

Our Mission

To provide quality and affordable education through effective teaching, individual attention, meaningful activities and continuous encouragement of students.
