OUR STORY

Education With Care

M.D Memorial School was established in 1997 at Aurangabad, Bulandshahr, Uttar Pradesh.

Our focus is on building strong academic foundations along with confidence, creativity and good values.

We believe every child learns differently. Therefore, students who need additional academic support receive extra attention and guidance.

English Medium Focus on confident communication and learning.
Smart Classes Technology-supported classroom learning.
Extra Support Additional academic guidance for students.
Co-Curricular Activities GK competitions, games, dance and singing.
