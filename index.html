ABOUT OUR SCHOOL

Growing Minds, Building Futures

M.D Memorial School is committed to providing quality education in a caring and supportive environment.

Education With Care

M.D Memorial School was established in 1997 at Aurangabad, Bulandshahr, Uttar Pradesh. Our focus is on building strong academic foundations along with confidence and creativity.

We believe every child learns differently. Therefore, children who need additional academic support receive extra attention and guidance.

English Medium Focus on confident communication and learning.
Smart Classes Technology-supported classroom learning.
Extra Classes Additional support wherever students need it.
Activities GK competitions, games, dance and singing.

Principal's Message

Our aim is to create an environment where every child feels encouraged to learn, grow and shine. We believe that education becomes meaningful when children receive knowledge, values and individual attention together.


— Mrs. Poonam Devi, Principal
